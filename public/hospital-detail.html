Hospital Overview
About West China Hospital

West China Hospital, affiliated with Sichuan University, is one of China's largest and most prestigious comprehensive hospitals. Founded in 1892, it has grown to become the premier medical center serving Western China with approximately 4,300 beds — reportedly the largest single-campus hospital in the world. The hospital handles complex cases referred from across the country and is consistently recognized among the top-tier institutions in national hospital evaluations. Its International Medical Center provides dedicated English-language services, international insurance direct billing, and full support for overseas patients seeking treatment in China.
