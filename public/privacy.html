ChinaMed Directory ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website at chinamed-directory.pages.dev ("the Website").

ChinaMed Directory is a static information directory. We do not:

Like most websites, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may automatically collect standard server log data, including:

The Website itself does not set cookies for tracking or advertising purposes. The only potential cookie usage is through our hosting provider (Cloudflare) for essential security and performance functions (such as DDoS protection). These are strictly necessary cookies that do not track users across sites.
