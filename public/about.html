About the Founder

Founded by a Frontline ICU Doctor in China

🩺
Founder · ICU Attending Physician
A Tertiary Public Hospital in Chengdu

This platform was initiated and personally built by a practicing physician from the intensive care unit (ICU) of a tertiary public hospital in Chengdu. Over a decade of frontline clinical rescue work gave him a deep understanding of how precise diagnosis and timely intervention determine a patient's quality of life. Long-term collaboration with specialists across Chinese hospitals — in traditional Chinese medicine, rehabilitation and acupuncture — also gave him firsthand appreciation of the strengths of integrated East-West care.

Crucially, because the founder is himself a practicing doctor within China's public health system, he knows intimately the departments, specialists, clinical pathways and referral rules of major hospitals nationwide. Once an overseas patient submits their medical history, he can draw on clinical experience to quickly judge which department and which type of specialist best fit the case — whether to prioritize Western surgical routes, focus on TCM rehabilitation, or combine both — so the patient reaches the right clinic directly, avoiding wasted time and money from wrong registrations or wrong departments after arrival.

In his clinical work he witnessed too many overseas patients miss the chance to seek care in China because of language barriers, unfamiliar procedures and information asymmetry — some arrived with complete records but no idea which hospital to approach; some were stranded by long appointment backlogs; some were even misled by unlicensed agents and found the department wrong upon arrival. These real pain points drove him to create this intermediary matching platform, using professional expertise to clear obstacles so that every trip to China for care goes straight to the point.

To be clear: this platform never replaces a doctor, and the founder will never provide any online consultation, prescription or remote treatment through the website. All functions of the platform are limited to information consultation for coming to China for care, pre-arrival document organization, and local ground-service coordination. Every diagnosis, examination and treatment must and can only be completed through an in-person consultation by a doctor licensed in China, after the patient arrives at a partner public hospital in Chengdu. What we do is build the bridge — not diagnose or prescribe.

China Today

Why Choose to Seek Care in China

From scarcity to world-class — a healthcare system worth traveling for.

A century ago, China's medical resources were extremely scarce amid war and poverty. Today, the land has been transformed beyond recognition. China has risen to the world's second-largest economy and achieved globally recognized accomplishments in modern medicine, traditional Chinese medicine, and infrastructure.

Today China has over 3,600 tertiary public hospitals (as of 2025), many equipped with internationally advanced CT, MRI and Da Vinci surgical robots. Meanwhile, tens of thousands of TCM clinics and standardized TCM departments form a unique dual-track system of precise Western diagnostics plus holistic TCM care. Chinese doctors rank among the world's busiest in annual outpatient and surgical volume — a wealth of hands-on case experience that no textbook can replace.

🏛️

A Mature Public-System Backbone

Chengdu hosts multiple nationally top-tier medical institutions such as West China Hospital of Sichuan University and Sichuan Provincial People's Hospital, plus strong specialized TCM hospitals — standardized care, transparent fees, and strict regulation.

🌿

Integrated East-West Advantage

Here you can receive evidence-based modern testing and treatment while simultaneously experiencing acupuncture, tuina, herbal steam therapy and dietary regulation — a synergy greater than the sum of its parts.

⚡

Minimal Waiting

At domestic public hospitals, general outpatient registration usually takes only 1–2 days, and most lab and imaging tests can be ordered and completed on the same day, greatly reducing the time cost of seeking care away from home.

🚇

Complete City Infrastructure

As a core city in western China, Chengdu has metro coverage across the city and an international airport with direct flights to dozens of major cities worldwide. From five-star hotels to budget apartments, from Sichuan cuisine to international supermarkets, every daily need is within reach — making the medical journey safe and comfortable.

Our mission is to build a professional, transparent and warm bridge, so that every overseas patient in need can smoothly walk into Chengdu's high-quality public hospitals and receive the safe, effective in-person diagnosis and treatment they deserve.
